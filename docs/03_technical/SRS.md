# Software Requirements Specification

## System Behavior

- The system shall reject unsupported or unsafe files.
- The system shall preserve an immutable source snapshot.
- The system shall route AI through bounded local adapters only.
- The system shall prevent direct AI mutation of manuscript content.
- The system shall support deliberate local publishing export only.

## Quality Attributes

- Privacy
- Integrity
- Reversibility
- Offline completeness
- Reviewability
